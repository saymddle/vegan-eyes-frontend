Chicken Parmesan Cutlet
Ingredients
* 1 lb. ground chicken or ground turkey
* 1 tsp kosher salt (Diamond Crystal kosher salt preferred)
* 1 tsp garlic powder
* 1 tsp onion powder
* All-purpose flour for shaping, as needed
* 2 eggs, whisked
* 1 full box panko breadcrumbs, use as needed — don't skimp
* Avocado oil spray
* ⅓–½ cup pizza sauce, use lightly
* 1 cup grated mozzarella
* 4 oz fresh mozzarella, sliced
* Fresh basil leaves
* Flaky sea salt
* Olive oil for finishing
Directions
Prep the Meat
* In a bowl, mix the ground chicken with salt, garlic powder, and onion powder until just combined.
* Let it sit for a few minutes while you prep.
Shape the Cutlet
* Lay out a large sheet of parchment paper and generously sprinkle it with flour.
* Place the meat on top and press into a very thin, large oval, like a pounded chicken cutlet. It should be large enough to nearly fill a sheet pan.
* Lightly sprinkle flour over the top surface.
Bread the First Side
* Brush the surface with egg wash.
* Cover completely with panko breadcrumbs, pressing gently so they adhere — no bare spots.
* Spray with avocado oil until the breadcrumbs are fully hydrated.
* They should look slightly saturated, not dry — this ensures they fry in the oven.
Flip and Bread the Second Side
* Place another large sheet of parchment on top and carefully flip.
* Remove the top parchment so the uncoated side is facing up.
* Brush with egg wash, then fully coat with panko, pressing gently.
* Spray again with avocado oil until the breadcrumbs are fully hydrated.
Bake and Crisp
* Lightly spray a large sheet pan with avocado oil.
* Carefully transfer the cutlet from the parchment onto the sheet pan.
* Bake at 450°F in a convection oven or air fryer for 15 minutes.
* Broil for 4–5 minutes, until deeply golden brown and crispy.
Build the Chicken Parmesan
* Remove from the oven.
* Spoon a thin layer of pizza sauce over the cutlet, leaving some crispy edges exposed.
* Sprinkle grated mozzarella evenly, then layer with fresh mozzarella slices.
Melt the Cheese
* Return to the oven.
* Bake at 425°F for 5–7 minutes until melted, or broil for 2–3 minutes until bubbly and lightly golden. Watch closely.
Finish
* Top with fresh basil.
* Drizzle lightly with olive oil and finish with flaky sea salt.
* Let sit for 1–2 minutes, then slice and serve.

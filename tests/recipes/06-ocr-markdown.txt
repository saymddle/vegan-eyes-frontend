# Chicken Alfredo
## Ingredients
- 2 boneless chicken breasts
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan
- 2 tbsp butter
- 12 oz fettuccine
## Instructions
1. Season and cook the chicken until golden, then slice.
2. Melt the butter, add the cream and simmer for 5 minutes.
3. Stir in the parmesan and toss with the fettuccine and chicken.
